How to win

  • Break bricks with the ball you own
  • First to 3 breaks wins the round
  • Or most breaks when the 1:00 timer ends
  • Clearing the board also ends the round
  • Timeout ties are possible
  • First to 2 rounds wins the match

Controls

  • WASD — move
  • Arrows / R-stick — angle (spring)
  • Space / A — jump
  • Jump again mid-air — pound
  • X / RT / LMB — spin (LTTP sweep)
  • RMB — snapback stance (aim with cursor)
